Amirhosein Farahmand

I'm a B.Sc. student in Computer Science at the University of Tehran. My research interests include medical artificial intelligence, probabilistic machine learning, generative modeling, multimodal learning, and survival analysis.

I am currently a Research Assistant working on clinical prediction modeling and survival analysis, collaborating with researchers at Stanford University, the University of Tehran, and the University of British Columbia. Previously, I was a Research Assistant at GISMA University of Applied Sciences, focusing on multimodal AI and Large Language Models.

I plan to further develop my algorithmic design and machine learning capabilities by pursuing an MSc in Computer Science at the University of Stuttgart.

My research focuses on developing technical methods grounded in probabilistic machine learning and medical AI to guide clinical workflows.

Robust Net Benefit Calculation for Survival Risk Prediction Models in the Presence of Informative Censoring
Amirhosein Farahmand, Mohsen Sadatsafavi, Abdollah Safari, Tae Yoon Lee
Society for Medical Decision Making (SMDM) Annual Meeting, Oslo, 2026   (Oral Presentation)
abstract / code

Developing and evaluating methods for estimating time-dependent net benefit for survival risk prediction models under informative censoring, covering both model-development and validation settings. Extended the framework to time-dependent and competing-risk outcomes.

Fact-Checking Sustainability Objectives Using Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation
Mohammad Mahdavi, Amirhosein Farahmand, Abolfazl Nadi
IEEE International Conference on Data Mining (ICDM), 2025
paper / code

Designed a multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system using large language models for claim verification across text and image inputs. Developed pipelines for evidence retrieval, multimodal analysis, and claim validation against a domain-specific knowledge base.
